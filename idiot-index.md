# Idiot index

Price of the finished part divided by the price of the raw stock in it.
High number means somebody is charging for machining, paperwork or habit.

Sorted worst first. Update when a quote comes in.
- index = part price / raw price

- bolt, index 61
- clamp, index 88. why is a clamp 88
- sheet metal screw, index 112
- o-ring, index 140
- harness, index 210
